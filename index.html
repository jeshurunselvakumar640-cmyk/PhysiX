Classical Mechanics Formulas

Mathematical foundations used by the PhysiX simulation engine

Projectile Motion Kinematics

Time of Flight (with elevation h₀): T = (v₀ sinθ + √(v₀² sin²θ + 2gh₀)) / g
Maximum Height (Peak Apex): H = h₀ + (v₀² sin²θ) / (2g)
Total Horizontal Range: R = d₀ + (v₀ cosθ) × T
Impact Speed at Touchdown: v_f = √(v₀² + 2gh₀)

Aim & Scientific Principle

Aim: To determine the Numerical Aperture (NA) and Acceptance Angle (θ_a) of an optical fibre using the output light spot divergence method on a graduated optical bench.

Apparatus: Optical fibre trainer kit, power supply, 650nm red laser diode, step-index optical fibre cable, precision NA measurement jig, white screen with concentric circles (1.0cm to 3.5cm), graduated bench scale.

Experimental NA Formula: NA = W / √(4L² + W²)
Waveguide Refractive Index Law: NA = √(n₁² - n₂²) = sin(θ_a)
Acceptance Cone Half-Angle: θ_a = arcsin(NA)
Mean Numerical Aperture: Mean NA = (1/N) ∑ NAᵢ

Theoretical Foundations

  • Optical Fibre: A cylindrical dielectric waveguide composed of a high-index Core (n₁) surrounded by a lower-index Cladding (n₂), where n₁ > n₂.
  • Total Internal Reflection (TIR): Light rays incident at angles greater than the critical angle θ_c = arcsin(n₂/n₁) undergo total reflection at the core-cladding boundary.
  • Acceptance Cone: The maximum cone of light rays incident on the fiber core face that will be guided through the fiber by TIR.
  • Diverging Output Spot: Upon exiting the fiber tip, the light beam diverges conically with half-angle θ_a, forming a circular spot of diameter W on a screen at distance L.
