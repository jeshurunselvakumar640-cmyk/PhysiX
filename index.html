Projectile Motion (Active Lab)

Flat Ground Range (h₀ = 0): R = (v₀² sin 2θ) / g (Symmetric: R(30°) = R(60°))
Time of Flight (with elevation h₀): T = (v₀ sinθ + √(v₀² sin²θ + 2gh₀)) / g
Maximum Height (Peak): H = h₀ + (v₀² sin²θ) / (2g)
Total Horizontal Range (General): R = (v₀ cosθ) × T
Impact Speed at Touchdown: v_f = √(v₀² + 2gh₀)

Simple Pendulum (Upcoming)

Oscillation Period (Small Angles): T = 2π √(L / g)
Angular Frequency: ω = √(g / L)

Collisions & Momentum (Upcoming)

Conservation of Momentum: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂
Coefficient of Restitution (e): e = (v₂ - v₁) / (u₁ - u₂)
